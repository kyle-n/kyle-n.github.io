Revitalizing _Bloodborne_ has been one of gaming's white whales for over a decade. From Software released their greatest masterpiece on PlayStation 4 in 2015, only to let it languish. They never created a sequel, ported it to PC, made it run at more than 30 frames per second, upscaled the textures or anything besides [one excellent expansion](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/The_Old_Hunters). Even the ex-chief of PlayStation [is baffled](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/even-former-playstation-chief-shuhei-yoshida-doesnt-know-why-bloodborne-hasnt-been-remastered-or-released-on-pc-its-a-complete-mystery/).

But AI changed things. Fans have used coding agents to make so many "PC ports" of _Bloodborne_, fans [can barely keep track of them all](https://www.reddit.com/r/BloodbornePC/comments/1x5n29i/heres_a_list_of_the_most_promising_ports_mainly/). These ports, which stand on the hard work of the [shadPS4](https://shadps4.net) emulator developers, have made the game easier to play than ever.

I spent a couple hours this week getting my own copy up and running on PC. It was difficult. But reader, when you see Yharnam in 4K and 60 FPS, it is _worth it_.

_Bloodborne_ is my favorite game. I've played it to completion six times. I've beaten every boss and seen every ending. It's a great game with amazing combat and an incredible setting. FromSoft's haunted version of London is up there with Rapture and the best worlds ever put in games.

And yet, I haven't played it for a few years. As perfect as _Bloodborne_ is, it can be inflexible and frustrating.

One, the health vials don't refill. _Dark Souls_ had the right idea - every time you die, your health items refill to max. Health flasks in _Bloodborne_, however, are a limited resource. Use too many, and you simply run out. Die too many times trying to learn a new boss? You have to stop fighting and go farm vials.

Two, there's no pause button. _Bloodborne_ can turn into a multiplayer game at any moment, so it makes sense. Still makes me annoyed.

Three, the game runs at 30 frames per second. This is fine and totally playable, but 60 is better.

Four, one of the weapons is unusable. The [Beast Claw](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/Beast_Claw) emits a horrible high-pitched whine when a [buff](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/Beasthood) is active. You can hear it in the background of [this guy's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TKP4_aNTUA4).

Five, you can't respec your character. You can complete the story, launch into new game plus, and be stuck using the exact same weapons and playstyle as before. It's frustrating to find a cool late-game weapon like the [Rakuyo](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/Rakuyo) and be unable to use it on your second playthrough because you can't change to a Skill build.

From Software must know these flaws are silly and unnecessary. All were fixed in _Elden Ring_, save the pause button.

But the reason I am playing _Bloodborne_ for the first time in 3 years is because on PC, we have _mods_. I don't have to deal with a single one of these problems.
